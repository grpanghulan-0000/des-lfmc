Laboratory Safety Induction

"People First, Environment Second, Property Third." — The core philosophy of the UPLB Materials Engineering safety response.

The "Prudent Person" Principle

As outlined in Section 3.1: If a specific hazard is not covered in the manual, you are expected to act in a careful and sensible manner. When in doubt, stop work and ask for guidance.

The Right to Know (Sec 1.5.1)

You have the right to access Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for all chemicals, operating manuals for heavy machinery, and emergency evacuation information.

Responsibilities (Sec 1.5.2)

Strict adherence to PPE, mandatory reporting of all incidents (Form 5), and environmental stewardship regarding chemical waste disposal.

Your classification dictates your access rights, exam validity, and required clearance forms.

Module 02 of 07

Technical Hygiene & Waste Segregation

Section 2.3 (Housekeeping), 3.3 (Hygiene) & 3.4 (Waste)

Fume Hoods (Sec 3.3.2)

The 6-Inch Rule

All work involving volatile or toxic substances must be performed at least 6 inches inside the hood for proper laminar airflow.

Sash Integrity

Never work with the sash raised above the certified safety mark. The sash serves as a physical shield against splashes and reactions.

Waste Segregation Map (Sec 3.4.1)

Click each waste stream to inspect its disposal protocol.

Never Return Rule

Tool Integrity (Sec 3.3.1)

To prevent violent reactions or stock contamination, never return unused chemicals to the original supply bottle. Never insert your own pipettes or spatulas directly into stock bottles; always pour a small amount into a clean secondary container.

Post-Lab & Sample Labeling (Sec 2.3)

Ensure furnaces and gas lines are turned off. Common areas must be left cleaner than found. Unlabeled containers will be discarded immediately. Required label format:

Name: Juan Dela Cruz

Sec: MatE 11 (T7)

Sem: 1st Sem 2026

Module 05 of 07

Emergency & Life Safety

Section 5.1 – 5.5 (Fire, Earthquake, Spills & Incident Reporting)

Fire Response (Sec 5.2)

  • Clothing Fire: Stop, Drop, and Roll. Use the safety shower immediately.
  • Extinguishers (PASS): Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep. Use Type ABC or Type D. Never use CO2 on a person (causes asphyxiation/frostbite).
  • Building Fire: Shout "FIRE!", turn off gas/furnaces if safe, evacuate, and close the door behind you.

Earthquake Protocol (Sec 5.3)

  • During: Drop, Cover, and Hold under a sturdy workbench. Stay away from glassware and heavy UTMs.
  • After: Shut down gas lines and furnaces immediately, then evacuate calmly.
  • Muster Point: Do not leave the DES area until the FIC accounts for your logbook "Time-In" as "Safe" (Sec 5.4).

Exposure & Spills (Sec 5.1)

Flush affected eyes or skin with copious water for a minimum of 15 minutes. For ingestion, do not induce vomiting unless instructed by the SDS.

For major spills (>1 Liter), evacuate the room, close the door, and notify the Lab Technician immediately.

Module 06 of 07

Administration, Forms & Penalties

Section 1.4 (Oversight), 2.4 (Penalties) & 4.3 (Forms)

Violations & Penalties (Sec 2.4)

1

First Offense: Verbal warning and immediate correction of the hazard.

2

Second Offense: Dismissal from the lab session and a "Laboratory Suspension" of 2 weeks (also applies to no-show bookings).

3

Third Offense: Permanent loss of laboratory access for the remainder of the semester.
